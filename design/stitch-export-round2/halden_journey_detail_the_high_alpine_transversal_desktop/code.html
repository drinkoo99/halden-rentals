CYCLE 01 // ASCENT STAGE IDENTIFIER: CH-GOT-01

Zurich Salon to Andermatt via Gotthard Tremola

3H 45M DRIVE • 185 KM • 37 HAIRPIN SWITCHBACKS

Depart the historic Zurich lakeside atelier heading south through Schwyz. The ascent culminates on the legendary cobbled switchbacks of the old Tremola road—a serpentine monument of 19th-century civil engineering leading over the Gotthard massif into the Urseren valley.

CYCLE 02 // THE CREST STAGE IDENTIFIER: CH-FUR-02

The Colossus: Furka Pass & Grimsel Granite

4H 15M DRIVE • 210 KM • RHONE GLACIER ICE CHAMBER

A relentless ascent into high drama. Pass the iconic Hotel Belvédère hanging above the Rhone Glacier ice tongue, then sweep down into Gletsch before tackling the monolithic granite walls of the Grimsel Pass, skirting deep turquoise hydroelectric reservoirs.

CURATED ACCOMMODATION // STAGE 02

Grimsel Hospiz

Historic granite mountain lodge perched above a sheer reservoir cliff. Features private subterranean vehicle vaults.

CYCLE 03 // DESCENT & SALON STAGE IDENTIFIER: CH-SUS-03

Sustenpass Crest & Return to Lucerne Salon

3H 30M DRIVE • 245 KM • LAKE URI FJORD DESCENT

Engineered in 1945 explicitly for grand touring flow, Sustenpass balances rhythm, broad radius sweeps, and dramatic tunnels chiseled through sheer stone. The drive gently winds down to the deep waters of Lake Uri and onto Lucerne for formal catalog return.

CURATED ACCOMMODATION // STAGE 03

Bürgenstock Resort & Alpine Spa

Private cliffside terminus. Valet mechanical inspection, luggage repatriation, and celebratory evening tasting.
